## Source-specific findings

- The NCERT Savitribai booklet contains conflicting death dates: its cover says 19 March 1897, while Hari Narake's essay says 10 March. The reviewed claim now states the conflict; a contemporary death record is still needed to settle it. Two claims in this source group remain pending because their required evidence was not checked.
- Ashoka's edicts are royal inscriptions. The reviewed wording reports what an edict says without automatically treating royal casualty figures, claimed reach, or religious experiences as independently established outcomes. One attribution was corrected from Pillar Edict III to Major Rock Edict III. Multiple modern translations of one inscription are not independent corroboration.
- Ramakrishna's Gospel and Order accounts support attributed traditions and recorded teachings. They do not independently prove private visions, verbatim dialogue, or a historical superlative. Supporting chapter URLs replace the exported contents-page URL in the decision evidence; staging database source links still require repair.

The new SQL function and distinct review-origin migration were applied only to staging, after the existing demand migrations; a local compatibility copy preserved the review origin during migration replay. The automatic admission evidence gates were not relaxed.

The **six real local-Supabase integration tests passed** across demand transactions and historical staging retrieval.
