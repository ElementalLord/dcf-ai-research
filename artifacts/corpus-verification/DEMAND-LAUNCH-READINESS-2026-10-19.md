## Launch blockers

1. Validate a small capped set of actual provider-driven Ask requests before enabling demand research: local answer, new fact, conflict, traditional/contested account, duplicate, inaccessible passage, provider error, and budget exhaustion. Inspect final user-visible citations and provider invoices. A separate explicit cost cap is needed first.
2. Diagnose the real-page false negative without weakening direct-passage support. The current extractor requires strong entity and term overlap and can miss a scholarly passage under a subject-identifying document title. Re-run the no-cost probe and negative controls after a targeted correction.
3. Review the remaining strict narrow-answer cases with real sources. The new fail-closed gate prevents the observed unrelated fallback, but broad answer quality and model-generated citation accuracy still need a launch sample.
4. Confirm production schema and data from a backup before any transition. The five demand/corpus migrations and isolated hold-gate logic must be staged as a reviewed production migration; this report does not assert they exist in production. Preserve 1,585 original claims, original source relationships, 319 decision records, and the hold register. Do not replay the 46-citation quarantine SQL blindly against a different baseline.
5. Set account-level provider spend limits and a request cap. Keep service-role credentials server-only. Verify function duration, robots/source access, licensing metadata, cache invalidation, and operational pause controls in a launch-like environment.

## Exact production configuration and later transition sequence

Required application setting: `EVIDENCE_POLICY_MODE=strict` explicitly. Keep `DEMAND_RESEARCH_ENABLED=false`, `DEMAND_VERIFICATION_SHADOW_ENABLED=false`, and `DEMAND_AUTO_ADMISSION_ENABLED=false` until separately authorized rollout stages. Keep the database research and admission pause controls available. Configure server-only Supabase and provider credentials, publisher access policy, and budget limits before any paid call; do not enable Brave storage rights without a rights basis.

After separate authorization: (1) capture a restorable production backup and baseline counts; (2) apply the three `2026101801/02/03` demand migrations, `20261019010000_strict_demand_admission_dedup.sql`, and `20261019020000_corpus_evidence_review_origin.sql` in order, plus a reviewed migration for the hold gate and guarded corpus dispositions; (3) assert all 1,585 originals and source rows remain, no undecided/excluded/held IDs or chunks are active, and caches are invalidated; (4) deploy compatible code with all demand flags off and strict explicit; (5) run local-only Ask/profile smoke tests; (6) enable bounded research for controlled traffic, then shadow verification; (7) inspect real source passages, citations, admissions, and spend; (8) enable automatic admission only after those gates pass and separate authorization.

Rollback: turn off automatic admission, shadow, and research first; pause database research/admission controls; move any newly auto-admitted claims out of active retrieval with their exclusive chunks inactive; clear answer and research caches; verify the preserved historical IDs and hold register; then revert compatible application code or restore the backup if a schema/data migration failed. Keep audit and source records for recovery.
