## Important qualities

- Operation Flood began in 1970 to scale the Anand pattern nationally through producer cooperatives, urban markets and dairy services. [S2] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- Phase III, 1985–96, strengthened procurement and marketing infrastructure and extended veterinary, feed and insemination services rather than simply creating new outlets. [S2] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- The cooperative’s move toward direct marketing mattered because farmers could capture more of the value between the village and urban consumer. [S1] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- The programme used village producer societies for milk procurement and member services, with processing, marketing and management coordinated at higher tiers. [S2] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_

## Main contributions

- Kurien's major contribution was institutional rather than solitary: he helped connect engineering, professional management, farmer ownership, processing, and national market infrastructure in the Anand pattern and Operation Flood. [S1] [S3] [S2] _(approved claim; human_manual)_
- After the Anand cooperative demonstrated its model, Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri backed a national board in 1965 to replicate it; the government’s sponsorship was central to scaling. [S1] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- At the Kaira union he helped establish processing capacity so members could handle milk beyond raw collection. [S1] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- IRMA was founded in 1979 to develop managers for cooperatives and other underserved rural organizations, showing how Kurien treated management talent as part of infrastructure. [S4] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- Kurien helped establish the Institute of Rural Management Anand in 1979 to train managers for rural producer organizations, extending the model beyond dairy hardware. [S1] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- Kurien served as founding chair of the National Dairy Development Board from 1965 to 1998, giving continuity to the national cooperative programme. [S5] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
