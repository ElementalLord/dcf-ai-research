## Main contributions

- The July 1980 SLV-3 launch placed Rohini RS-1 in orbit and established an indigenous Indian satellite-launch capability. [S6] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- The presidential profile credits Kalam with helping shape the later Indian launch vehicle programme, especially the PSLV configuration. [S1] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- The SLV-3 programme developed a four-stage solid-propellant launch vehicle able to place small payloads in low Earth orbit. [S6] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- Kalam advocated PURA, or Providing Urban Amenities in Rural Areas, as a development model for village clusters. [S7] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- Kalam described education as learning from teachers, libraries, and laboratories, a view that joined classroom knowledge with practical inquiry. [S8] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- Kalam's PURA proposal joined physical, electronic, and knowledge connectivity to expand rural economic opportunity. [S7] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
