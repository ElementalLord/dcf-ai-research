## Contemporary relevance

- Brihadisvara remains a major surviving material record of high Chola art and architecture and anchors Rajaraja’s long cultural legacy. [S8] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
- The Brihadisvara complex has been protected by the Archaeological Survey of India since 1922 and is now part of the UNESCO-listed Great Living Chola Temples. [S5] _(draft; awaiting human decision; ai_candidate)_
