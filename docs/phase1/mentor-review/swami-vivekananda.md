## Important qualities

- At the Parliament's final session, Vivekananda rejected the idea that interreligious harmony required conversion to a single faith. [S4] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- He proposed attention and introspection as tools for studying inner experience. [S5] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- He taught that spiritual methods should suit differing human temperaments rather than be forced into one form. [S6] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- He urged each tradition to learn from others while preserving its own identity. [S4] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_

## Life and historical/social context

## Leadership and relational characteristics

- According to Belur Math, Ramakrishna entrusted Narendra with organizing the young disciples into a monastic order. [S1] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- Belur Math dates Narendra's meeting with Ramakrishna at Dakshineshwar to November 1881 and recounts his direct questioning of the teacher. [S1] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- Belur Math recounts Narendra asking Ramakrishna whether he had seen God and presents Ramakrishna's response as pivotal; this is the Order's account of their relationship. [S1] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- Belur Math recounts that Narendra questioned God's existence before meeting Ramakrishna, rather than accepting spiritual claims uncritically. [S1] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_

## Contested material meeting the inclusion standard

- Vivekananda explicitly credited Ramakrishna for his spiritual teaching, while later scholars debate whether his philosophy is best read as classical Advaita, neo-Vedanta, or a more original synthesis. [S3] [S8] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_

## Contemporary relevance

- Vivekananda established Belur as the permanent centre for the monastic order associated with the Ramakrishna Math. [S1] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- Vivekananda founded the Ramakrishna Mission on 1 May 1897 to organize teaching and service by monks and lay members. [S1] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- His account of service joined sympathy for vulnerable people with a duty to resist hatred and fanaticism. [S9] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
- In his India lectures, Vivekananda called for education that builds capacity and character. [S10] _(approved claim; corpus_evidence_review)_
