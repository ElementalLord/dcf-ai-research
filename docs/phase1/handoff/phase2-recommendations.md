# Phase 2 recommendations

These recommendations begin after Phase 1 deployment and smoke testing. They do not reclassify ordinary Phase 1 acceptance work as future development.

## High priority

1. **Formalize editorial roles and approval records.** Productionize researcher, reviewer, Mentor, and administrator permissions; preserve named, dated decisions and immutable review history.
2. **Strengthen thinner evidence areas.** Use existing readiness and evidence-gap tools to prioritize approved-source coverage for sparse framework sections and carefully reconsider withheld material only when evidence improves.
3. **Harden production operations.** Add monitored deployment, backups, error/usage visibility, provider cost limits, secret rotation, and documented incident/recovery ownership.
4. **Apply deferred database work deliberately.** Review uncommitted or deferred migrations, especially evaluation storage, against the live schema before applying them; never infer migration state from local files alone.
5. **Accessibility and performance hardening.** Audit with assistive technology and real devices, establish performance budgets, and address findings from production traffic and smoke tests.

## Medium priority

1. **Expand beyond 25 personalities through the established pipeline.** Add people only with framework fit, credible evidence, human review, Mentor sign-off, and an explicit public-category decision.
2. **Restore or extend the broader category plan.** Introduce remaining original categories when DCF approves their frameworks and has enough balanced profiles to present them well.
3. **Improve cross-personality exploration.** Develop reviewed thematic and relationship data rather than relying only on shared category/lens similarity.
4. **Clean research duplicates.** Review exact and near-duplicate sources, passages, and claims with provenance-preserving merge procedures.
5. **Productionize selected Resources workflows.** Refine onboarding, readiness, review queues, and audit reporting based on actual staff use.

## Optional

1. **Improve Ask and retrieval.** Evaluate grounded answer quality, abstention, citations, latency, and cost before presenting open-ended Q&A as a production service.
2. **Deeper thematic experiences.** Build curated journeys across qualities, historical contexts, institutions, traditions, or leadership patterns using reviewed structured data.
3. **Alternative applications.** Reuse the portable export in educational materials, exhibits, mobile experiences, or a future frontend without coupling them to the current implementation.
